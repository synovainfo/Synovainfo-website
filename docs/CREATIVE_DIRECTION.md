# Synova Infotech — Creative Direction & Art Direction Guide

## 1. Visual Mood & Aesthetic Philosophy
- **Core Aesthetic**: Dark Luxury Corporate with Aurora Glass Accent highlights.
- **Visual References**: Apple Enterprise, Stripe, Vercel, Linear, NVIDIA, IBM.
- **Lighting**: Subtle radial gradients and aurora glow orbs shining through translucent frosted glass (#0F1B2D / #080E18).

## 2. Color Palette & Psychology
- **Deep Slate / Navy (#080E18, #0A1220, #0F1B2D)**: Represents Security, Stability, and Enterprise Authority.
- **Electric Cyan (#06B6D4) & Accent Blue (#2563EB)**: Represents Velocity, Multi-Cloud Connectivity, and High-Speed APIs.
- **Emerald (#10B981)**: Represents 99.999% Operational Health, ROI, and Zero-Trust Compliance.
- **Deep Purple (#8B5CF6)**: Represents Autonomous AI Infrastructure.

## 3. Typography & Editorial Layout Style
- **Heading Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Geist Display — Bold, Magazine-scale headline composition with asymmetrical 60/40 alignment.
- **Body Font**: Inter / Geist Sans — Crisp readability, generous line heights (1.6x - 1.75x).

## 4. Materials, Glass & Micro-Interactions
- **Glassmorphism Layering**: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px); background: rgba(15, 27, 45, 0.75); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12);`
- **Cursor Physics**: Smooth spring physics (`stiffness: 400, damping: 25`).
