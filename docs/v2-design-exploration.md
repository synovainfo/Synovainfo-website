# Synova Website V2 Design Exploration

## Scope

Major public surfaces reviewed: Header, Navigation, Hero, Services, Industries, Process, Case Studies, Technology, Footer, and public utility pages.

## Five Design Concepts

1. Command Center
   - Strengths: Strong enterprise credibility, natural fit for dashboards, telemetry, cloud infrastructure, and executive CTAs.
   - Weaknesses: Can become generic cybersecurity if overused.
   - Score: Usability 9, Originality 8, Enterprise 10, Accessibility 9, Responsive 9.

2. Editorial Boardroom
   - Strengths: Premium magazine rhythm, high trust, suited to executive messaging and case-study storytelling.
   - Weaknesses: Needs custom diagrams to avoid becoming static.
   - Score: Usability 9, Originality 9, Enterprise 9, Accessibility 10, Responsive 9.

3. Systems Blueprint
   - Strengths: Memorable engineering language, excellent for architecture, process, technology, and services.
   - Weaknesses: Can feel too technical for first-time business visitors.
   - Score: Usability 8, Originality 9, Enterprise 10, Accessibility 9, Responsive 9.

4. Global Infrastructure Atlas
   - Strengths: Strong fit for industries, delivery hubs, compliance, and scale.
   - Weaknesses: Home and services need a stronger conversion layer.
   - Score: Usability 8, Originality 8, Enterprise 9, Accessibility 9, Responsive 8.

5. Precision Studio
   - Strengths: Distinctive luxury, high whitespace, premium typography.
   - Weaknesses: Risks under-communicating engineering depth unless paired with operational visuals.
   - Score: Usability 9, Originality 9, Enterprise 8, Accessibility 10, Responsive 10.

## Selected Direction

Selected concept: Command Center + Editorial Boardroom + Systems Blueprint.

Reason: This hybrid gives Synova a recognizable enterprise language: executive narrative, technical proof, and operational visualizations. It supports all page types without repeating card grids.

## Refinement Iterations

Iteration 1: Increased editorial asymmetry and removed generic centered hero patterns.

Iteration 2: Replaced logo-heavy technology presentation with system topology and pipeline visuals.

Iteration 3: Reworked service and industry sections into presentation rows, maps, rails, and diagrams instead of repeated cards.
